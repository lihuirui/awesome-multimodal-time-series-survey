\subsection{Cross-Modal Patch Reprogramming Frameworks}
The foundational breakthrough in adapting pre-trained NLP backbones to time series without catastrophic forgetting was achieved via cross-modal reprogramming:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{One Fits All (GPT4TS)~\cite{Zhou2023OneFitsAll}:} Zhou et al. demonstrated that by freezing the multi-head self-attention and feed-forward layers of a pre-trained GPT-2 backbone while tuning only the linear input projection, layer normalization parameters, and an output head, the model exhibits remarkable universal pattern-matching capabilities across forecasting, classification, anomaly detection, and imputation.
    \item \textbf{Time-LLM~\cite{Jin2023TimeLLM}:} Jin et al. extended this paradigm by recognizing that numerical time series inherently lack semantic context. Time-LLM introduces: (1) Prompt-as-Prefix, which encodes dataset-specific background knowledge, task specifications, and statistical summary statistics (trends, lags) into textual prompts; and (2) a multi-head cross-attention Patch Reprogramming Layer that projects input patches into the text embedding dictionary.
    \item \textbf{AutoTimes~\cite{Liu2024AutoTimes}:} Overcomes the fixed-horizon limitation of previous forecasters by casting time series as an in-context autoregressive generation task, conditioning on textual chronological timestamps to capture long-range calendar periodicities.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Modality Transcoding: The Visual Rendering Paradigm}
A radical departure from language-based adaptation is the \textit{Visual Transcoding} framework pioneered by VisionTS~\cite{Chen2024VisionTS}. Rather than struggling to map continuous 1D temporal trajectories into 1D text tokenizers, VisionTS reformulates forecasting as an image reconstruction task:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item The normalized historical lookback window and future horizon are rendered onto a 2D canvas as a continuous line chart.
    \item A pre-trained Visual Masked Autoencoder (MAE)~\cite{Chen2024VisionTS} treats the historical curve as visible image patches and the future forecast segment as masked patches.
    \item Strikingly, because natural image pre-training on ImageNet instills strong priors over spatial continuity, edge tracing, and geometric smoothness, VisionTS achieves competitive zero-shot forecasting performance without requiring any domain-specific time series fine-tuning.
\end{enumerate}
Expanding on visual synthesis, Time-VLM~\cite{Zhong2025TimeVLM} combines visual time-frequency spectrogram representations with textual descriptions and raw temporal sequences, demonstrating that visual rendering and textual guidance provide complementary inductive biases.

\subsection{Conversational Multimodal LLMs \& Reasoning Suites}
Moving beyond classical regression metrics, recent research aims to empower foundation models with temporal reasoning capabilities:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{ChatTS~\cite{Zhao2024ChatTS}:} Identifies that text serialization loses fine-grained numerical resolution while naive reprogramming lacks conversational flexibility. ChatTS implements native numerical tokenization coupled with a synthetic \textit{Time Series Evol-Instruct} curriculum, enabling multi-turn dialogues, peak detection, and causal hypothesis formulation.
    \item \textbf{TimeOmni-1~\cite{Tan2025TimeOmni1}:} Formulates the \textit{Time Series Reasoning Suite (TSR-Suite)}, structuring temporal intelligence across three hierarchical stages: \textit{Perception} (identifying seasonality, trends, and change points), \textit{Extrapolation} (forecasting and scenario generation), and \textit{Decision-Making} (optimizing interventions based on temporal dynamics).
\end{itemize}
