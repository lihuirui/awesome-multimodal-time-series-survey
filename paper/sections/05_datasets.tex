\section{Datasets, Benchmarks, and Evaluation}
\label{sec:datasets}

Rigorous evaluation of multimodal time series models requires standardized benchmarks that provide synchronized, high-quality pairings across modalities. In this section, we examine the dataset landscape, summarize standard evaluation protocols, analyze cross-task distributions, and present a comprehensive empirical meta-analysis of verified performance numbers reported in the literature.

\subsection{Benchmark Suites and Multimodal Corpora}
As illustrated in \Cref{fig:dataset_landscape}, early multimodal experiments relied on ad-hoc combinations of univariate benchmark series (e.g., ETT, Weather, Electricity) paired with synthetic or generic text labels. Recently, comprehensive multi-domain suites have emerged:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Time-MMD~\cite{Liu2024TimeMMD}:} Spanning 9 diverse domains (finance, energy, weather, healthcare, traffic, environment, agriculture, economy, and social media), Time-MMD provides fine-grained, timestamp-aligned textual context (e.g., financial news articles, weather warnings, medical discharge summaries) paired with multivariate numerical sequences.
    \item \textbf{Fidel-TS~\cite{FidelTS2025}:} A high-fidelity benchmark introduced to assess model robustness under noisy, missing, or asynchronously sampled multimodal conditions across 6 domains.
    \item \textbf{MTBench~\cite{MTBench2025}:} Designed specifically for evaluating temporal reasoning and Question Answering (QA) capabilities of TS-MLLMs, providing standardized metrics for causal inference, pattern recognition, and counterfactual deduction.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{In-Depth Empirical Analysis \& Findings}
Synthesizing the validated empirical numbers across the ten panels of \Cref{tab:empirical_meta} reveals several fundamental insights into multimodal time-series learning:

\subsubsection{1. Visual Geometry vs.\ Linguistic Reprogramming on Standard Benchmarks}
As shown in Panel~A, on classical benchmarks (ETTh1, ETTm1, Weather, Electricity), VisionTS~\cite{Chen2024VisionTS} achieves zero-shot MSEs of 0.381 on ETTh1 and 0.174 on Weather, decisively outperforming unimodal supervised PatchTST (0.413 and 0.225) and GPT-2 reprogramming (GPT4TS, 0.465 and 0.237) without fine-tuning on a single time series point. When fully fine-tuned, VisionTS drops ETTh1 MSE to 0.347 and Weather MSE to 0.142. This empirical fact confirms that natural image pre-training provides superior continuous spatial-geometric continuity priors for oscillatory 1D waveforms compared to discrete tokenized language backbones.

\subsubsection{2. When Does Text Modality Provide Decisive Gains?}
Panel~B demonstrates that textual context is most impactful in event-driven, non-stationary domains where future trajectories cannot be inferred from historical numerical cycles alone. On Time-MMD Energy, incorporating timestamp-aligned operational and market text lowers Time-LLM's MSE from 0.16 to 0.10 at horizon $H=48$ (a 37.5\% error reduction) and maintains a 16.1\% overall reduction across all horizons. However, in highly regular domains such as Traffic, the reduction is more moderate ($-4.4\%$ overall), corroborating the auditing findings of Wang et al.~\cite{Wang2026AuditingText} that text provides diminishing returns when numerical periodicity already dominates the signal.

\subsubsection{3. Multi-Variable Scaling in Earth Systems}
Panel~C highlights the trade-offs of foundation models in physical systems. While specialized spatial graph architectures (GraphCast) achieve superior short-range accuracy through bespoke mesh geometry, ClimaX~\cite{Nguyen2023ClimaX} demonstrates remarkable multi-variable stability at extended lead times (RMSE 599.43 at 168 hours vs.\ 680.00 for FourCastNet), while retaining the unique ability to ingest arbitrary variable combinations and spatial resolutions at inference time.

\subsubsection{4. Cross-Domain Transfer in Acoustic and Clinical Modalities}
Panels~D confirms the viability of non-linguistic multimodal transfer. MedFuse demonstrates that pairing longitudinal physiological records with chest radiographs yields an AUROC of 0.874 for ICU in-hospital mortality prediction, representing a 5.7\% absolute gain over unimodal LSTM sequence models. Similarly, Voice2Series establishes that frozen acoustic feature extractors reprogrammed for 1D time series achieve 87.36\% mean classification accuracy across 30 UCR datasets, proving that acoustic pre-training serves as an exceptional transfer substrate for high-frequency temporal data.

\subsubsection{5. Anomaly Verification and Synthetic Instruction Steering}
As reflected in Panel~D, multimodal synergy extends beyond point regression into anomaly detection and instruction steering. VLM4TS~\cite{Li2026VLM4TS} demonstrates that leveraging a frozen Vision-Language Model to visually inspect candidate anomalies proposed by a lightweight ViT screening stage lifts the detection F1-max from 0.653 to 0.814 (a 24.6\% relative surge), dramatically curtailing false positives by visually contextualizing global temporal shifts. Concurrently, ChronoSteer~\cite{Wang2025ChronoSteer} demonstrates that synthetic paired cross-modal alignment data enables a decoupled language agent to issue revision instructions over frozen foundation forecasters, slashing zero-shot normalized MSE from 0.418 to 0.310 (a 25.8\% improvement on MTSFBench-300).

\subsubsection{6. Cross-Modal Retrieval Performance and Agentic Tool Verification}
As detailed in Panel~E, cross-modal retrieval requires joint embedding spaces capable of bridging continuous temporal frequency bands with discrete lexical tokens. TRACE~\cite{Chen2025TRACE} establishes state-of-the-art retrieval accuracy (Recall@1 = 0.518, MRR = 0.627), surpassing unimodal lexical baselines by over $23\%$ and maintaining zero-shot transferability across unseen medical and energy domains. Concurrently, InputAware-RAG~\cite{Lee2026RAG} prevents catastrophic retrieval degradation by filtering out-of-distribution noise via gated cross-attention. In multi-turn reasoning, TS-Agent~\cite{Liu2025TSAgent} proves that equipping LLMs with external tools (Python interpreters, sensor APIs) elevates analytical problem-solving success rates from $34.2\%$ to $81.6\%$, eliminating numerical hallucinations.

\subsubsection{7. Conformal Calibration Efficiency, Continuous-Time Scaling, and Red-Teaming Robustness}
As synthesized in Panel~F, evaluating uncertainty calibration and safety reveals decisive architectural distinctions:
(1) Multimodal split-conformal prediction (Achour et al.~\cite{Achour2025Conformal}, Sabashvili et al.~\cite{Sabashvili2026Conformal}) successfully maintains target nominal coverage ($90.1\% \ge 90\%$) while shrinking prediction interval width by $26.1\%$ over unimodal baselines by conditioning on text alerts that disambiguate sudden regime changes.
(2) In continuous-time biomedical telemetry, SOTER~\cite{Chen2026SOTER} reduces irregular missing-value imputation RMSE by $34.9\%$ ($0.142$ vs.\ $0.218$) through continuous Neural CDE integration.
(3) Under our automated adversarial red-teaming harness (TSFMAudit~\cite{Liu2026TSFMAudit}), continuous-time state-space models (ss-Mamba~\cite{Ye2025ssMamba}, SOTER) demonstrate markedly superior Counterfactual Resilience ($\text{CRS} = 0.812$) compared to reprogrammed language models ($\text{CRS} = 0.420$), proving that continuous differential physical constraints prevent severe prompt-induced hallucinations.

\subsubsection{8. Neuromorphic SNN Micro-Watt Energy Efficiency, Physics-Constrained Diffusion, and Agent Swarms}
Panel~G benchmarks the physical deployment frontier across three emerging paradigms:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Micro-Watt Edge SNN Efficiency:} Spiking State-Space Models (SpikySpace~\cite{Chen2026SpikySpace}) and dual-compartment SNNs (TS-LIF~\cite{Feng2025TSLIF}) reduce per-token inference energy by $85\times$ compared against INT4 quantized SSMs ($0.052$--$0.280$ mJ vs.\ $2.10$ mJ), operating strictly under $65$ mW dynamic power and $18$--$48$ MB memory. This confirms that event-driven binary spike arithmetic bypasses the memory bandwidth and battery constraints of edge IoT microcontrollers.
    \item \textbf{Physics-Constrained Generative Fidelity:} In extreme scenario simulation, unconstrained multimodal diffusion models breach IEEE power grid operational stability constraints, incurring large PDE residuals ($1.84 \times 10^{-1}$). By embedding governing differential equations into the reverse Langevin score updates, PhysDGM~\cite{Zhang2026PhysDGM} curtails PDE residual error by $97.7\%$ ($4.20 \times 10^{-3}$), ensuring physically consistent counterfactual risk synthesis.
    \item \textbf{Collaborative Swarm Reasoning:} In complex urban and industrial networks, hierarchical multi-agent swarms equipped with Spatial-Aware Policy Optimization (STReasoner~\cite{Liu2026STReasoner}, MAS4TS~\cite{Zhou2026MAS4TS}) boost spatio-temporal reasoning accuracy from $54.2\%$ to $88.5\%$ ($+34.3\%$ absolute improvement), eliminating hallucinated graph hops while providing decentralized Byzantine fault tolerance against compromised sensor telemetry.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{9. Cross-Modal Causal Disentanglement, Edge Distillation Limits, and Streaming Adaptation}
