\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Time series data represents the temporal pulse of our interconnected physical, biological, and socio-economic systems. Over the past several decades, the study of temporal sequence modeling has evolved across three major technological waves: classical statistical and autoregressive modeling (e.g., ARIMA, exponential smoothing), task-specific deep neural architectures (e.g., RNNs, LSTMs, TCNs, Informer, PatchTST), and most recently, large pre-trained Time Series Foundation Models (TSFMs)~\cite{Goswami2024MOMENT,Liu2024Timer,Ansari2024Chronos,Ekambaram2024TTM}. 

Despite extraordinary advances in univariate and multivariate numerical modeling, an essential constraint persists across unimodal paradigms: \textit{real-world temporal dynamics do not unfold in an informational vacuum}. When a sudden traffic congestion spike occurs, an electrical grid frequency deviates, or a stock price undergoes rapid volatility, the underlying generative causes are rarely confined to historical numerical values alone. Instead, these phenomena are profoundly shaped by external multimodal signals: breaking news reports, meteorological alerts, natural language operational dispatches, visual monitoring streams, and relational graph topologies.

\subsection{The Rise of Multimodal Time Series Intelligence}
The unprecedented general reasoning capabilities of pre-trained Large Language Models (LLMs) and Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have fundamentally transformed artificial intelligence. Researchers quickly observed that the sequence modeling principles governing language and vision exhibit deep mathematical parallels with time series. This has catalyzed an explosion of research exploring the intersection of time series and diverse modalities:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Cross-Modal Reprogramming:} Groundbreaking frameworks such as Voice2Series~\cite{Yang2021Voice2Series}, One Fits All (GPT4TS)~\cite{Zhou2023OneFitsAll}, and Time-LLM~\cite{Jin2023TimeLLM} demonstrated that frozen pre-trained backbones (acoustic encoders, GPT-2, LLaMA) can be repurposed as high-accuracy time-series learners by projecting temporal patches into non-temporal semantic feature spaces.
    \item \textbf{Visual Transcoding \& Vision-Language Synergy:} Counter-intuitively, VisionTS~\cite{Chen2024VisionTS} and its continual variant VisionTS++~\cite{Chen2025VisionTSPlus} revealed that reformulating numeric series as line-chart images allows off-the-shelf visual masked autoencoders (MAE pre-trained on ImageNet) to perform competitive zero-shot forecasting without any domain fine-tuning. Concurrently, Time-VLM~\cite{Zhong2025TimeVLM} unites visual, textual, and temporal representations for augmented forecasting.
    \item \textbf{Physics-Informed and Planetary Multimodality:} In climate and geosciences, foundation models such as ClimaX~\cite{Nguyen2023ClimaX}, Prithvi WxC~\cite{Schmude2024PrithviWxC}, Aurora~\cite{Bodnar2024Aurora}, and SeisT~\cite{Zhang2024SeisT} fuse heterogeneous physical sensor variables, multi-level atmospheric grids, and seismic waveforms, scaling foundation models to planetary-scale observations.
    \item \textbf{Clinical and Multi-Sensory Cross-Modality:} In clinical healthcare, frameworks like MedFuse~\cite{Hayat2022MedFuse} combine longitudinal physiological ICU time series with synchronous chest radiograph (CXR) visual features to optimize in-hospital mortality prediction and clinical phenotyping.
    \item \textbf{Conversational Reasoning \& TS-MLLMs:} Pioneering systems such as ChatTS~\cite{Zhao2024ChatTS}, TimeOmni-1~\cite{Tan2025TimeOmni1}, ChatTime~\cite{ChatTime2024}, and Time-MQA~\cite{Shen2025TimeMQA} elevate time-series models from passive function approximators to interactive reasoning agents capable of answering temporal questions, conducting counterfactual reasoning, and explaining anomalies.
    \item \textbf{Multimodal Datasets \& Benchmarks:} Systematic benchmarks such as Time-MMD~\cite{Liu2024TimeMMD}, Fidel-TS~\cite{FidelTS2025}, and MTBench~\cite{MTBench2025} have begun curating high-fidelity, aligned cross-modal time series corpora across diverse real-world domains.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Need for a Dedicated Survey}
While prior comprehensive surveys have examined large foundation models for pure time-series or spatio-temporal data~\cite{Jin2023LargeModelsSurvey,Jin2024PositionLLMTS}, and conceptual discussions have highlighted potential multimodal opportunities~\cite{Zhang2025HowCan}, there lacks a systematic survey providing deep architectural breakdowns and empirical meta-analyses of \textit{explicitly multimodal time series modeling}—spanning text-guided forecasting, vision-based line-chart transcoding, acoustic reprogramming, physics-informed Earth models, and conversational reasoning agents.

\subsection{Contributions of This Survey}
To fill this critical void, this paper provides:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{A Systematic, Verifiable Review:} Conducted under the PRISMA 2020 framework spanning 2021 to 2026 (\Cref{fig:prisma_flow}), where every cited paper is verified against authoritative bibliographic APIs.
    \item \textbf{A Four-Pillar Taxonomy:} A taxonomy deconstructing multimodal time series research along Modality Pairing, Fusion Architecture, Non-TS Role, and Downstream Tasks (\Cref{fig:taxonomy}).
    \item \textbf{Deep Methodological Analysis:} A unified comparative dissection of cross-modal reprogramming, visual line-chart transcoding, acoustic adaptation, contrastive alignment, and interactive reasoning architectures.
    \item \textbf{Empirical Meta-Analysis \& Benchmark Synthesis:} An extensive empirical meta-table comparing validated performance metrics across standardized long-term benchmarks, multi-domain aligned text-TS benchmarks (Time-MMD), Earth system prediction (WeatherBench), and clinical fusion.
    \item \textbf{Critical Perspectives on Open Challenges:} A rigorous appraisal of the modality gap, benchmark data contamination, temporal granularity mismatches, text sensitivity auditing, and future research trajectories.
\end{itemize}
